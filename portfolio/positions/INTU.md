## Thesis

Intuit is a durable SaaS compounder built on three interlocking moats: QuickBooks (SMB accounting with deep accountant × client × integration network effects), TurboTax (tax preparation with proprietary return data moat), and Credit Karma (consumer credit marketplace fed by 140M+ member tax and financial data). The platform flywheel — financial data from taxes → credit decisions → SMB lending → back to QuickBooks — compounds with each user cohort. Intuit Assist (AI layer across all products) monetizes the proprietary data stack that competitors cannot replicate. Positioned at 7% as a quality, GAAP-profitable SaaS compounder replacing the speculative P position.

## Bull case

- QuickBooks: ~7M SMB subscribers (per FY2024 earnings); deeply embedded in workflows via 600K+ accountant partners and 750+ app integrations. Switching cost is a full migration of financial history.
- Tax data moat: decades of return data trains TurboTax and Credit Karma models. IRS cannot replicate product depth (guided prep, live expert, full service) within Direct File's scope.
- Credit Karma: 140M+ members; monetizes via credit cards, personal loans, auto, home lending marketplace. Revenue diversification across economic cycles.
- Intuit Assist AI (launched FY2024): embedded across QuickBooks, TurboTax, Credit Karma — extracts value from proprietary data without incremental data acquisition cost.
- GAAP profitable; consistent earnings beats; ~13–15% revenue CAGR (per FY2024 earnings guidance); dividend grower (~0.5% yield, unverified).

## Bear case & risks

- IRS Direct File expansion: free federal filing program piloted in 2024 (25 states for FY2025, unverified). Could reduce TurboTax DIY TAM over time if scope expands to complex returns.
- SMB macro sensitivity: QuickBooks subscriber growth slows in recessions as small businesses churn or downgrade.
- Valuation premium: ~30–35x forward P/E (unverified), pricing in durable compounding. De-rates sharply if growth slows below ~10%.
- Credit Karma fintech exposure: personal loan and BNPL origination volume tied to credit cycle; down materially in rate-hike environments (per FY2023 Credit Karma revenue decline disclosed in 10-K).
- Competition in SMB: Xero, FreshBooks, Sage compete internationally; QuickBooks international growth slower than domestic.
