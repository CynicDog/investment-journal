## Description

Bostick v. Intuit Inc. (Case No. 3:26-cv-01444, S.D. Cal., filed ~2026-03-31) alleges TurboTax's zero-interest Refund Advance loans charged active-duty military borrowers fees that breach the Military Lending Act's 36% MAPR cap. Defendants include Intuit, Credit Karma (CK Progress Inc.), and four partner banks. Case is in early stage — no certified class, no settlement. The complaint implicates both TurboTax and Credit Karma's banking-product infrastructure, two material revenue drivers. Sources: The College Investor https://thecollegeinvestor.com/78787/intuit-faces-class-action-over-turbotax-refund-advances-to-active-duty-military/; Top Class Actions https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/intuit-class-action-claims-tax-refund-advance-loans-violate-military-lending-act/
