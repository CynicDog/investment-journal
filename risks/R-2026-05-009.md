## Description

Intuit announced a ~17% global workforce reduction (~3,000 employees) on 2026-05-20 alongside Q3 FY2026 earnings, concurrent with new multi-year Anthropic and OpenAI AI partnership disclosures. The scale and compressed timeline (U.S. last day 2026-07-31) create execution risk: competitive talent loss in AI/fintech hiring market, morale drag on remaining workforce, and restructuring charges burdening GAAP FY2026 EPS. Q3 press release and exact restructuring charge size not yet indexed at time of recap. (Seeking Alpha / Reuters, 2026-05-20: https://seekingalpha.com/news/4595309-intuit-to-cut-about-17-percent-of-its-workforce-report)
