## Description

S&P 500 forward earnings yield ~4.7% vs 10-year Treasury ~4.51% as of 2026-06-01 — equity risk premium approximately +0.17%, near the lowest reading since the dot-com era (Axios, 2026-05-27). VOO fwd P/E ~21.2x vs 5-year avg ~19.9x and 10-year avg ~18.9x (FactSet/GuruFocus). At this ERP level, any negative earnings surprise or yield spike could trigger a de-rating in the index.

## Monitor for

10-year Treasury yield crossing 4.7%+; S&P 500 fwd P/E compressing below 19x on earnings cuts; FOMC June 16-17 statement; May CPI print (June 10).
