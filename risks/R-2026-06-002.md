## Description

Autonomous AI agents are displacing human software users, placing structural downward pressure on per-seat SaaS licensing revenues across major IGV holdings (ADBE, CRM, and adjacent names). Gross margin compression is expected as companies transition from seat-based to consumption/outcome pricing. ADBE and CRM are reported ~50% below early-2025 levels; IGV NAV total return YTD 2026 is -9.52% (BlackRock/iShares). The 25% portfolio weight in IGV amplifies the impact of continued revenue-model disruption on overall portfolio performance.

## Monitor for

MSFT, ORCL, ADBE, CRM revenue growth acceleration; software gross margin stabilisation; IGV NAV recovery above 2025 lows; any accelerated shift to consumption pricing disclosures on earnings calls.
